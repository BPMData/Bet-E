# Style 1: 1960s Kodachrome Test-Kitchen Photo (`kodachrome-1960s`)

Classic *Betty Crocker* / *Good Housekeeping* step-by-step picture cookbook look, anchored in warm 1963 Kodachrome/Ektachrome studio flash photography.

## Visual Signature
- **Imagery:** Full-bleed edge-to-edge 1960s cookbook photographs on an avocado-green boomerang Formica countertop with harvest-gold and orange accents, cream stoneware bowls, coral-pink nails, and crisp white blouse cuffs.
- **Paper & Palette:** Warm cream paper (`#f3e7cc`) with halftone print grain, brick red (`#b5321f`), mustard (`#d49a1c`), avocado (`#6f7d2e`), and chestnut brown (`#6b3a1e`).
- **Typography:** *Abril Fatface* display titles, *Yellowtail* script taglines, *Oswald* tracked kickers/subheads, and *Libre Caslon Text* body copy.
- **Layout:** Page 1 features a tilted taped `3:4` hero photo beside a drop-cap intro, green-bordered ingredient box, 3-tile mustard glance strip, and rotated `"Kitchen Tested & Approved"` seal; Page 2 presents a 3×3 grid of numbered `4:3` step photos plus `"Make It Ahead"` / hint cards.
