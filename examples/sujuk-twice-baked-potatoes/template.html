Twice-Baked Potatoes

Makes 8 Halves or 4 Boats400°F, Then 375°FAbout 1 Hr. 20 Min.

Mounded high, bubbling with aged white cheddar, nutty Gruyère and Parmigiano, these spuds practically steal the show from the turkey! Crisp bits of spiced beef sujuk and bright sweet Hungarian paprika crown every forkful.

Twice-baked potatoes have always been a hostess's secret weapon: assemble them a day ahead, and they emerge from the Thanksgiving oven looking like a million dollars. Ours spare no luxury—sweet roasted garlic, nutty browned butter, velvety cream cheese, tangy sour cream, three cheeses and savory crisped sujuk.

You Will Need

Sujuk, Potatoes & Roasted Garlic

  • 5–6 oz. beef sujuk, casing removed. Grate 1 oz. on a box grater for garnish; finely dice the rest (¼-in. cubes)
  • 4 large russet potatoes (8–10 oz. each, similar size), scrubbed and dried
  • 1 tbsp. olive oil (or rendered sujuk fat), plus kosher salt, for skins
  • 1 head garlic, plus 1 tsp. olive oil

Dairy & Cheese

  • 4 tbsp. unsalted butter, browned
  • 4 oz. full-fat cream cheese, cubed and softened
  • ½ cup full-fat sour cream, room temperature
  • 2 tbsp. heavy cream, warmed, plus up to 2 tbsp. more (only if needed)
  • ¾ cup sharp white cheddar, freshly grated (½ cup in, ¼ cup on top)
  • ¾ cup Gruyère, freshly grated (½ cup in, ¼ cup on top)
  • ¼ cup Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated (2 tbsp. in, 2 tbsp. on top)

Herbs & Seasoning

  • ⅓ cup fresh chives, finely sliced, plus extra for garnish
  • 1 tsp. fresh thyme leaves, finely chopped (optional)
  • ¼ tsp. kosher salt to start, then adjust to taste
  • ½ tsp. black pepper  ·  a pinch grated nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp. sweet (Hungarian) paprika, for dusting

Picture-Perfect Method

See how easy it is! Step by Step

Follow each photograph closely, and your Thanksgiving side dish will look straight out of the test kitchen.

1

Crisp the Sujuk

Cook diced sujuk in a cold, dry skillet over medium-low heat, stirring often, 4–6 min. until crisp. Drain on paper towels; save 1 tbsp. fat for skins. Keep grated sujuk raw and chilled.

2

Roast & First Bake

Heat oven to 400°F. Slice tops off garlic, oil and wrap in foil. Prick unwrapped potatoes 6–8 times; rub with oil or sujuk fat and salt well. Bake together on middle rack (foil pan below) 50–60 min. Cool 10–15 min.; lower oven to 375°F.

3

Butter & Cheese

Grate all three cheeses from the block and set aside—bagged cheese won't melt smoothly. Separately, melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat until foamy, nutty and flecked with golden specks (3–4 min.). Pour butter into a bowl at once.

4

Scoop the Shells

Halve potatoes lengthwise (8 halves), or slice off the top third (4 boats). Scoop the steaming flesh into a large bowl, leaving ¼-inch walls so shells hold their shape. Set shells on parchment-lined sheet pans.

5

Food Mill

While the potato is still hot, crank it with the squeezed-out roasted garlic through the food mill's medium plate in 2 batches. Turn forward, then a quarter-turn back to clear; scrape the underside now and then.

6

Mix the Filling

With a spatula, stir in browned butter and cream cheese, then sour cream and 2 tbsp. warm cream (add more by the tbsp. only if needed). No mixer! Fold in filling cheeses, crispy diced sujuk, chives, thyme, salt, pepper and nutmeg. Taste first.

7

Stuff & Bake

Mound filling high and rough up peaks with a fork. Sprinkle with topping cheeses. Bake uncovered at 375°F for 20–25 min. until golden and bubbling. Broil 1–2 min. for deeper color; watch closely.

8

Garnish & Serve

While baking, crisp grated sujuk in a dry skillet 1–2 min.; drain. Out of the oven, dust tops lightly with sweet paprika through a sieve or from up high so it stays bright red. Scatter crispy sujuk and extra chives; serve piping hot.

Make It Ahead

1–2 days ahead: Finish through the cheese topping in Step 7, don't bake. Cool, cover loosely and chill; keep grated garnish sujuk raw and separate.

Thanksgiving Day: Stand 30–45 min., then bake uncovered at 375°F for 30–40 min. until 165°F inside. Fry garnish sujuk, dust paprika and serve!
