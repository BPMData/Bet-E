Crispy Sujuk
Twice-Baked Potatoes

Three-Cheese & Roasted Garlic  ·  Yields 8 Halves or 4 Boats

Kitchen Tested 400° / 375° About 1 Hr. 20 Min.
Table Note: Mounded sky-high with aged white cheddar, nutty Gruyère, Parmigiano, sweet roasted garlic, browned butter and crisp nuggets of spiced beef sujuk—finished with a bright shower of sweet Hungarian paprika.

Twice-baked potatoes are the holiday hostess's sharpest trick: prepare them up to two days ahead, then slip them into the oven while the turkey rests so they arrive at the table bubbling, golden and fragrant.

Pantry Checklist

4-Potato Batch

Sujuk, Potatoes & Garlic

  • 5–6 oz. beef sujuk, casing removed (grate 1 oz. for garnish; finely dice rest into ¼-in. cubes)
  • 4 large russet potatoes (8–10 oz. each), scrubbed & dried
  • 1 tbsp. olive oil or rendered sujuk fat, plus kosher salt
  • 1 head garlic + 1 tsp. olive oil

Dairy & Three Cheeses

  • 4 tbsp. unsalted butter, browned
  • 4 oz. full-fat cream cheese, cubed & softened
  • ½ cup full-fat sour cream, room temperature
  • 2 tbsp. heavy cream, warmed (+ up to 2 tbsp. if needed)
  • ¾ cup sharp white cheddar, grated (½ cup in, ¼ cup top)
  • ¾ cup Gruyère, grated (½ cup in, ¼ cup top)
  • ¼ cup Parmigiano-Reggiano (2 tbsp. in, 2 tbsp. top)

Herbs & Seasoning

  • ⅓ cup fresh chives, finely sliced + extra for garnish
  • 1 tsp. fresh thyme leaves, chopped (optional)
  • ¼ tsp. kosher salt to start · ½ tsp. black pepper · pinch nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp. sweet (Hungarian) paprika, for dusting

Step-by-Step Picture Method

Follow the numbered ink plates below—everything comes together smoothly with zero electric mixers.

1

Crisp the Sujuk

Cook diced sujuk in a cold, dry skillet over medium-low heat, stirring often, 4–6 min. until crisp. Drain on paper towels; save 1 tbsp. fat. Chill raw grated sujuk.

2

Roast & First Bake

Heat oven to 400°F. Cut tops off garlic, oil and wrap in foil. Prick potatoes 6–8 times; rub with oil or sujuk fat and salt well. Bake unwrapped on middle rack 50–60 min. Cool 10–15 min.; lower oven to 375°F.

3

Butter & Cheese

Grate all three cheeses from the block and set aside—bagged cheese won't melt smoothly. Separately, melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat until foamy, nutty and flecked with golden specks (3–4 min.). Pour butter into a bowl at once.

4

Scoop the Shells

Halve potatoes lengthwise (8 halves), or slice off the top third (4 boats). Scoop steaming flesh into a large bowl, leaving ¼-inch walls so shells stand tall. Arrange shells on parchment-lined pans.

5

Food Mill

While the potato is still hot, crank it with squeezed-out roasted garlic through the food mill's medium plate in 2 batches. Turn forward, then a quarter-turn back to clear; scrape underside now and then.

6

Mix the Filling

With a spatula, stir in browned butter and cream cheese, then sour cream and 2 tbsp. warm cream (add more by tbsp. only if needed). No mixer! Fold in filling cheeses, crispy diced sujuk, chives, thyme, salt, pepper and nutmeg.

7

Stuff & Bake

Mound filling high and rough up peaks with a fork. Sprinkle with topping cheeses. Bake uncovered at 375°F for 20–25 min. until golden and bubbling. Broil 1–2 min. if desired.

8

Garnish & Serve

While baking, fry grated sujuk in a dry skillet 1–2 min. until crisp; drain. Out of the oven, dust tops lightly with sweet paprika through a sieve or from up high. Scatter crispy sujuk and chives; serve piping hot!

Hostess Shortcut

Make It Ahead

1–2 days ahead: Complete Steps 1–7 through the cheese topping, but don't bake. Cool, cover loosely and refrigerate; keep grated sujuk raw and separate.

Thanksgiving Day: Stand 30–45 min., then bake uncovered at 375°F for 30–40 min. until 165°F inside. Fry garnish sujuk, dust paprika and serve!
